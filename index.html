Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work? +
We charge at the end of the month based on your exact usage. If you are on the Developer tier and don't exceed the limit, you will never be charged.
Can I use my own data to train models? +
Yes, Pro and Enterprise users can upload custom datasets to fine-tune our base models in a secure, isolated environment.
What is your data privacy policy? +
We strictly do not use customer API data or fine-tuning datasets to train our public models. Your data belongs to you.
